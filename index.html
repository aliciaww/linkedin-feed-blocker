Safari extension

Keep LinkedIn useful.
Hide the feed.

LinkedIn Feed Blocker removes the algorithmic home feed, while messages, jobs, search, profiles, and networking stay available.

↗ Turn it on or off from the LinkedIn Feed Blocker button in Safari’s toolbar.

What it does

  • Hides the home feed on linkedin.com.
  • Keeps the parts of LinkedIn you came to use.
  • Lets you switch feed blocking on or off whenever you want.

How it works

  1. Open the companion app once and enable the extension in Safari Settings.
  2. Visit LinkedIn and click the extension button in Safari’s toolbar.
  3. Use the switch to hide or show your home feed.

Private by design

The extension runs on your device. It has no tracking, analytics, remote code, or data collection. One local setting remembers whether feed blocking is enabled.
